Why FEFO?

Food safety depends on using the right stock at the right time. FEFO prioritises the nearest expiry date; FIFO prioritises the earliest received batch.

FEFOReduces expiry risk
FIFOTracks arrival order

Local-first by design

This version runs on your computer and saves to data/inventory.json. It has no login or multi-user database, so do not expose it directly to the public internet. For multiple staff or multiple devices, add authenticated hosting and a managed database first.
